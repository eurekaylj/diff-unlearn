Diffusion model computations use bundled source in code/local_diffusion.
Installed diffusers/transformers supply configuration, model base classes,
loading mixins, logging, backend utilities and tokenization.
The bundled branch covers Stable Diffusion 2 UNet/VAE/DDIM/CLIP text encoder;
other diffusion architectures and optional adapters are outside its scope.
Pretrained weights and model configuration assets are not included.
